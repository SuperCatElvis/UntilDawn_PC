## What's new in 0.3.2

### Fixed: black squares and flickering on AMD graphics cards

Reported in [issue #5](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/issues/5) by **manuelnunes2004-cmyk** on an
AMD Radeon RX 7800 XT: with 0.3.1 most of the screen was black and flickering, while the "Preparing shaders" screen
looked fine.

The fix was found and contributed by **[Pedro-Castro31](https://github.com/Pedro-Castro31)** in the same issue and is
included unchanged. Thank you! At the end of every frame the program copies the finished picture to the window and
fills the borders around it with black. On the way there:

- the last drawing pass of the frame could still be open while the copy was recorded, which Vulkan does not allow;
- nothing made sure the frame's drawing had finished and was visible before the copy read it;
- the black fill and the copy wrote to the same picture with nothing ordering them, so the black could land on top
  of the copied frame.

NVIDIA's driver happened to run these in the right order; AMD's does not have to. The fix closes the drawing pass
first, waits for the frame's drawing before copying it, orders the black fill before the copy, and tells the
program's resource tracking that the copy reads the frame. It adds no AMD-only switch and no extra waiting on the
CPU. Pedro-Castro31 found the faults with the Khronos synchronization validation layer, tested the fix on an
RX 7800 XT (Adrenalin 26.10.1) and in offscreen tests on an RX 5700 XT, and reports that the affected RX 7800 XT
player confirmed the game now displays correctly.
On the development PC (NVIDIA) the picture is unchanged: four recorded test scenes look the same as with 0.3.1 (the
few pixels that differ also differ between two runs of 0.3.1 itself).

### Fixed: the stranger's clothing disappearing (Chapter 5)

Parts of the stranger's coat vanished a few seconds after he appeared: the two thin strips hanging from it in every
session, and his waist in about half of them.

- **Cause:** his coat is simulated cloth (Havok Cloth, part of the game). Each cloth point is stored as four numbers,
  its x, y and z position and a fourth "spare" number that is meant to stay at 1 and is never used for the cloth's
  shape. The cloth solver also nudges the spare number, but nothing pulls it back, so tiny rounding differences grew
  every frame wherever the cloth was squashed, until the number overflowed to infinity. A later step of the
  simulation multiplies it by zero, which turns infinity into "not a number", and that reached the bones that move
  the coat's pieces - so those pieces disappeared.
- **Fix:** before each cloth update the program sets the spare number back to 1. The cloth's shape and movement do
  not depend on it: recorded cloth from three scenes (36 cloth simulations, from small strips to 2,900-point cloth)
  was simulated with and without the fix, and every position came out bit for bit identical.
- **Checked in play:** the stranger was whole in all 4 encounters played with the fix (before it, the strips were
  missing in all 14 sessions and recordings we have). The same runaway number was found in other cloth in other
  chapters too; the fix covers all of the game's cloth. In other chapters played with 0.3.2, all 55 cloth
  simulations in three recordings were intact.
- For comparison, the developer option `--no-cloth-fix` turns it off.

## Known issues

- The **memory corruption** behind most crashes is not fixed yet. Saves are kept; start the game again.
- **Frame rate** is below 30 FPS in the heaviest scenes.
- **Shaders not in the list** (other routes through the game) can still pause the first time they appear; they are
  prepared from the next start on.
- Reported and **not yet investigated**: the lantern jumping when it hits a table (Chapter 5), the skin of Hannah's
  ghost, and strong coloured lens-flare spots from a bright window in the sanatorium boiler room.
- Tested on the development PC (NVIDIA); the AMD fix was tested by its author on an RX 7800 XT and an RX 5700 XT.
  Reports from AMD and Intel GPUs are especially welcome.

Full list: [Known issues](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md)

## Updating from 0.3.1

Copy the new `UntilDawn.exe`, `compat\ud_compat.exe` and the text files into your 0.3.1 folder (`pipelines.udm` is
unchanged). **Do not overwrite** `Config\settings.ini`, `savedata\` or `Game\` (your settings, saves and game).
Saves, settings and `Cache\` carry over; as after every update, the first start shows the "Preparing shaders" screen
once more. From 0.3.0 or earlier, see the
[installation guide](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md#updating-from-an-earlier-release).

**Download check:** the SHA-256 of `UntilDawn_0.3.2.zip` is given on the release page and in `UntilDawn_0.3.2.zip.sha256.txt`.

## Getting started

1. Download **`UntilDawn_0.3.2.zip`** below and extract it to a folder you can write to.
2. Put your game files in the `Game` folder (so that `Game\eboot.bin` exists), or set `game_dir` in
   `Config\settings.ini`.
3. Start **`UntilDawn.exe`**. The first start prepares the shaders (about 2 minutes on the development PC, longer
   with fewer CPU cores).

Full instructions: [Installation guide](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md)
· [Controls](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/CONTROLS.md)

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- CPU with AVX, F16C, BMI1, LZCNT, POPCNT and MOVBE (Intel Haswell / AMD Ryzen or newer)
- 16 GB memory recommended; keep the Windows page file on
- GPU with a current Vulkan 1.3 driver (NVIDIA tested; AMD: see above; Intel untested)
