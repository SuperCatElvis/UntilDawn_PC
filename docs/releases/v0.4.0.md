v0.4.0 is a large graphics and performance update. The game now runs at your monitor's own resolution in
borderless full screen, renders noticeably faster (especially at 1440p and 4K), prepares its shaders much faster on
the first start, and fixes several long-standing graphics problems, including characters' clothing that vanished.
Optimised Rendering (Fast Mode) is now on by default, and the frame-rate limit can be chosen separately.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No game files are included.** You still need your own legally obtained copy of Until Dawn (2015) for PS4,
> **CUSA02636**, with update **1.03** applied. The program checks your game files at every start.

> [!WARNING]
> This is still a **work-in-progress** release. Expect occasional crashes, graphical issues and stutters.

## Fixed

- **Characters' clothing no longer vanishes.** Simulated cloth (for example the stranger's coat and Mike's clothing)
  could break and disappear in some scenes. The wind input to the cloth now behaves as on the PS4, and cloth that still
  goes wrong is restored automatically instead of disappearing.
- **Dr Hill's face** (the red patch on his chin in the menu and his scenes) now looks as it should; the same correction
  also applies to some lighting.
- **Falling snow, mist and light shafts** no longer disappear when you stand still.
- **Softer, correct skin shading** on faces is back (it was missing in earlier versions).
- Physics and cloth now keep real time at high frame rates (120, 240 and uncapped) instead of speeding up.
- Stability improvements in the renderer and in memory handling.

## Improvements

- **Native resolution with borderless full screen (new default).** The game fills your monitor at its own resolution:
  1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3840x2160 and others. Alt+Enter switches between borderless full screen and a window. The
  picture always keeps its 16:9 shape (black bars on ultrawide or 16:10 monitors, never stretched). Menus, text and
  videos are scaled correctly at every size.
- **Choose the resolution if you want:** `render_resolution = auto` (default) or a fixed `1080p`, `1440p`, `2160p`.
  On graphics cards with little video memory the resolution is lowered automatically.
- **Optimised Rendering (Fast Mode), on by default.** The game starts its next frame while the graphics card finishes
  the previous one, with safety checks that keep the game's memory consistent. Same picture, clearly faster. It can be
  turned off with `optimised_rendering = 0` in `Config\settings.ini`.
- **Frame-rate limit choices:** 30, 60 (default), 120, 240 or uncapped (`frame_rate_limit` in `Config\settings.ini`).
  Uncapped still stops at 240 FPS so that physics and cloth stay correct. The frame-rate limit and Optimised Rendering
  are independent: changing one never changes the other.
- **Your settings are kept when you update.** An existing `Config\settings.ini` keeps your values; the new settings are
  added with their defaults. If your file still had the old default window (1280x720 windowed), it switches to the new
  borderless full screen; a window size you chose yourself is kept.
- The shader preparation screen can be closed safely at any time, and the shader cache is saved before the game
  starts, so loading is not slowed down by it.
- Settings file: clearer explanations for every setting.
- The log (`Logs\UntilDawn.log`) now records your GPU and whether Optimised Rendering is active, to help with bug reports.

## Performance

- **Better performance, especially at 1440p and 4K.** Depth and copy work on the graphics card is much cheaper. On the
  development PC (RTX 3090 Ti) frame times in a demanding scene dropped by about 7% at 1440p and 15% at 4K compared
  with the previous development builds, and Optimised Rendering adds more on top. Results vary with your PC.
- **Much faster first-run shader preparation.** Preparation now uses all processor threads and only prepares what your
  current configuration needs. On a modern 8-core (or larger) CPU it takes about 2-3 minutes; it can take longer on
  slower CPUs or when other programs (for example antivirus scans or downloads) are busy. Later starts take only a
  few seconds.
- The shader list is read in a few seconds instead of up to a minute or more on a busy PC.

## Known Issues

- **Main menu:** the character's look-around in the main menu moves too fast at high frame rates. Gameplay is not
  affected.
- **Brief dark lighting at the start of some scenes** (for example the start of the Chapter 5 grate cutscene); it
  corrects itself after a moment.
- **First-start shader preparation time depends on your CPU** and on what else is running; it is shown on screen and
  happens once (again after a graphics driver update or a new version).
- **Occasional brief stutters** when entering new areas.
- Optimised Rendering (Fast Mode) has been tested mostly on NVIDIA graphics cards. If you see problems on AMD or Intel,
  try `optimised_rendering = 0` and please report the result.
- The memory corruption behind most of the remaining crashes is not fixed yet. Saves are kept; start the game again.

## Updating from v0.3.2

Copy the new `UntilDawn.exe`, `compat\ud_compat.exe` and the text files into your v0.3.2 folder (`pipelines.udm` is
unchanged). **Do not overwrite** `Config\settings.ini`, `savedata\` or `Game\`. Your saves, settings and game files
carry over; the new settings are added to your `settings.ini` on the first start. As after every update, the first
start shows the "Preparing shaders" screen once more.
