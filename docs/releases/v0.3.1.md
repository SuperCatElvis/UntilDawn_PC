# Until Dawn Native PC Recompilation – v0.3.1

**Tag:** `v0.3.1` · **Asset:** `UntilDawn_0.3.1.zip`

> [!WARNING]
> This is still an **early work-in-progress test release**. Expect bugs, crashes, graphical problems and pauses.
> v0.3.1 is a focused graphics and shader update on top of 0.3.0: see-through and distance-faded surfaces are drawn
> correctly (the black icicles, the solid liquid of the "strange skull" clue, the squares on the cable car windows),
> draws that were left out in parts of Chapters 5 and 6 are drawn, and the shader list now comes from a full
> playthrough. The **memory corruption behind most crashes is not fixed yet**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No game files are included.** You need a decrypted dump of the original PS4 Until Dawn (**CUSA02636**) with
> the **01.03** update, made from your own **legally obtained** copy of the game. This download does not contain
> the game.

## What's new in 0.3.1

Everything in 0.3.0 is kept (GPU coherence, the chapter-load crash fix, the optional Halloween Hunt). Figures are from
the development PC (RTX 3090 Ti, Core i9-12900KF, 64 GB).

### Graphics

- **Fixed: see-through and distance-faded surfaces.** A pixel shader can ask the graphics hardware for its pixel's
  distance from the camera. The PS4 hardware gives the distance itself; the program's shader translator gave
  1 ÷ distance. Everything that fades or tints by depth was affected (about 4 in 10 of the game's pixel shaders read
  this value), for example:
  - the **black icicles** - on the lodge in the prologue they are now see-through, as on the PS4 (the black icicles
    reported in Matt and Emily's scenes are the same kind of surface; not re-checked there yet);
  - the **"strange skull" clue** in the sanatorium - the liquid in the jar was solid and hid the skull; now it is
    clear, checked against a PS4 screenshot of the same clue;
  - the **squares on the cable car's frosted windows** in Chapter 1;
  - a **pale glow along the edges of hair** (Hannah in the prologue; most likely also Sam's glowing hair in the psycho
    chase, not re-checked there yet).

  Checked on 14 recorded scenes from across the game, each with and without the fix.
- **Fixed: draws left out in parts of Chapters 5 and 6.** Some of the game's shaders there need 33 resource slots,
  one more than NVIDIA graphics cards accept the way the program passes them, so those draws were skipped (about
  8,500 to 9,000 per hour of play in those chapters). They now use the ordinary way of binding resources. Checked on
  recorded scenes with every draw sent that way; the scenes that need it have not been seen in play with the fix yet.

### Shader preparation

- **The shader list now comes from a full playthrough:** `pipelines.udm` holds **6,797** shader combinations (0.3.0:
  4,440), recorded in six play sessions from the prologue to the ending (with the earlier lists). In that playthrough
  0.3.0's list had 88.7% of what the game needed ready before it started, and about 2.8 minutes of compiling happened
  during play; 96% of the combinations it met for the first time during play are now in the list. Another route
  through the game (other choices) can still meet some that are not in it; 108 combinations found in the playthrough
  could not be included. As before, the list holds no game code: it says where each shader is in your own game files,
  and the program translates it again from them.
- **The first start takes a little longer:** about **2¼ minutes** on the development PC with nothing cached
  (2 minutes 8 seconds and 2 minutes 19 seconds in two tests; 0.3.0's list: 1 minute 45 seconds). Later starts prepare in under a second. Esc or Circle still
  skips it; whatever is skipped is compiled during play.

### Smaller changes

- Very long sessions: the table that spots identical shaders under different names is four times larger (after
  about an hour of play it was full, and identical pipelines were made again - no visible effect).
- The shader summary at the end of `Logs\UntilDawn.log` now says how many of its "new" pipelines were distinct and
  how many were the same pipeline made again.

## Known issues

- The **memory corruption** behind most crashes is not fixed yet. Saves are kept; start the game again.
- **Frame rate** is below 30 FPS in the heaviest scenes.
- **Shaders not in the list** (other routes through the game) can still pause the first time they appear; they are
  prepared from the next start on.
- Reported in the playthrough and **not yet investigated**: the lantern jumping when it hits a table (Chapter 5), the
  skin of Hannah's ghost, and strong coloured lens-flare spots from a bright window in the sanatorium boiler room.
  The blue light by the sanatorium door was checked against a PS4 screenshot of the same spot and matches it.
- Tested on the development PC only (NVIDIA). Reports from AMD and Intel GPUs are especially welcome.

Full list: [Known issues](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md)

## Updating from 0.3.0

Copy the new `UntilDawn.exe`, the new `pipelines.udm`, `compat\ud_compat.exe` and the text files into your 0.3.0
folder. **Do not overwrite** `Config\settings.ini`, `savedata\` or `Game\` (your settings, saves and game). Saves,
settings and `Cache\` carry over; the first start shows the "Preparing shaders" screen once for the new list. From
0.2.1 or earlier, see the
[installation guide](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md#updating-from-an-earlier-release).

**Download check:** the SHA-256 of `UntilDawn_0.3.1.zip` is given on the release page and in `UntilDawn_0.3.1.zip.sha256.txt`.

## Getting started

1. Download **`UntilDawn_0.3.1.zip`** below and extract it to a folder you can write to.
2. Put your game files in the `Game` folder (so that `Game\eboot.bin` exists), or set `game_dir` in
   `Config\settings.ini`.
3. Start **`UntilDawn.exe`**. The first start prepares the shaders (about 2 minutes on the development PC, longer
   with fewer CPU cores).

Full instructions: [Installation guide](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/INSTALLATION.md)
· [Controls](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/blob/main/docs/CONTROLS.md)

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
- CPU with AVX, F16C, BMI1, LZCNT, POPCNT and MOVBE (Intel Haswell / AMD Ryzen or newer)
- 16 GB memory recommended; keep the Windows page file on
- GPU with a current Vulkan 1.3 driver (NVIDIA tested; AMD tested with 0.1.3; Intel untested)

## Help wanted: hardware testing

Reports from **AMD and Intel GPUs** and from **laptops with two GPUs** are especially welcome. Run
