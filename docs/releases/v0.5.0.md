# Until Dawn PC Recompilation — v0.5.0

**Release date:** 25 November 2026

This update introduces additional language support, expanded regional compatibility, an automatic updater, improved audio, memory optimisations and various graphical and stability fixes.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **No game files are included.** You need your own legally obtained copy of Until Dawn (2015) for PS4 — **CUSA02636** or **CUSA00194** — with update **1.03** applied.

## Fixed

* Fixed audio crackling, playback issues and clipping, particularly at higher resolutions.
* Fixed incorrect pages appearing in Dr Hill's book.
* Fixed DualShock 4 and DualSense motion-control drift during "Don't Move" sequences.
* Fixed thin graphical lines appearing at resolutions above 1080p.
* Fixed a startup crash caused by thread allocation conflicts with overlays and other applications ([#9](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/issues/9)).
* Fixed a rare startup issue caused by Windows memory allocation conflicts.
* Fixed a rare cloth simulation crash during Chapter 5.
* Fixed a startup issue affecting the CUSA00194 regional edition.

## Improvements

* Added the Language Importer (`UDLanguageImporter.exe`), which imports languages from another regional copy of Until Dawn that you own:
  * from CUSA00194: French, German and Italian (voice acting and text), Dutch and Turkish (text);
  * from CUSA02636: Spanish, Portuguese, Russian and Polish (voice acting and text), Danish, Finnish, Norwegian and Swedish (text).
* Added support for CUSA00194 (Europe 1) with update 1.03 as an alternative to CUSA02636.
* Added experimental compatibility with additional regional editions, including CUSA00359 (Americas).
* Improved language configuration, including all 20 original languages, language codes, regional availability and voice-acting support.
* Added an automatic updater, built into `UntilDawn.exe` and enabled by default. It checks for new releases at startup, asks before installing, verifies downloads and supports rollback. Your game files, saves, settings and language packs are never modified. Set `update_check = 0` to turn it off.
* Added a Depth of Field setting, allowing depth of field, bokeh and motion blur to be disabled.
* Added compatibility options for older processors.
* Improved compatibility diagnostics and startup error messages.
* Improved configuration management, automatically adding new settings while preserving existing player preferences.
* `UntilDawn.exe` shows the original Until Dawn icon again, in Explorer as well as on the window and taskbar.

## Performance

* Improved texture management on graphics cards with less than 12 GB of VRAM, reducing unnecessary texture reloads.
* Added automatic low-memory optimisations, reducing graphics driver memory allocation on systems with limited available memory.
* Reduced memory usage on systems with limited memory.
* Improved automatic rendering batch allocation based on available VRAM.
* Performance is the same as or slightly better than v0.4.1 at 1080p, 1440p and 4K.

## Known Issues

* Occasional crashes caused by memory corruption may still occur.
* Systems with 16 GB of RAM may still experience crashes in demanding scenes.
* Performance may drop below 60 FPS in more demanding areas.
* Brief graphical issues may occur following a camera transition during the Chapter 5 stranger sequence.
* Compatibility issues affecting some AMD graphics cards remain under investigation ([#15](https://github.com/SuperCatElvis/UntilDawn_PC/issues/15)).
* Additional regional editions and their associated language packs remain experimental.

See `KNOWN_ISSUES.txt` for the full list.
