Until Dawn native PC port - controls (WIP 0.2.0)
================================================

Controllers
-----------
Controllers are read only while the game window has focus. Keyboard and mouse keep working alongside them.

DualSense (PS5), DualSense Edge, DualShock 4 (PS4) - USB or Bluetooth, no extra software needed:
  every button works as on the PS4, including the touch pad (swipes and click) and the motion sensors
  (the "Don't move" moments: hold the controller still). Tested: DualSense.
  Not supported yet: vibration and the light bar on these controllers.

Xbox-style controllers (XInput; also DS4Windows / Steam virtual pads), vibration works:
  A = Cross        B = Circle        X = Square        Y = Triangle
  LB / RB = L1 / R1                  LT / RT = L2 / R2
  Left / right stick = sticks        stick clicks = L3 / R3
  D-pad = D-pad    Menu (Start) = Options              View (Back) = touch pad click
  No touch pad swipes and no motion sensors on these controllers: use the keyboard keys F / G / R / V for swipes;
  "Don't move" moments count the controller as held still.

Keyboard and mouse
------------------
  Left stick           W A S D
  Right stick (camera) I J K L
  Cross                Enter or Space
  Circle               Esc or Backspace
  Square               Q
  Triangle             E
  L1 / R1              1 / 3
  L2 / R2              Z / C
  D-pad                Arrow keys
  Options              Tab
  Touch pad click      T
  Touch pad swipes     F = left, G = right, R = up, V = down
  Touch pad finger     hold the left mouse button inside the window and drag
  L3 / R3              not available on the keyboard (use a controller)
  The keys cannot be remapped yet.

Mouse aiming (M turns it on and off):
  mouse movement       right stick (camera / aim)
  left click           R2 (shoot)
  right click          L2 (aim)

Skipping movies
---------------
The intro logos, the "Previously on Until Dawn" recaps, the butterfly-effect tutorial, the movie that opens the
prologue and the Chapter 1 bus ride can be skipped: press any button to show the Skip prompt in the bottom-right
corner, then press Cross (Enter / Space) while it is shown. The rest of the recap is skipped with it.
